Even From Far Away...

Sometimes, I hate the distance because I just want to be beside you.

I want to see your face, hear your voice beside me, hold your hand, and give you a big hug whenever I miss you.

There are days when I wish I could just teleport to you instead of missing you from far away.

❤️

But even with the distance between us, I still choose to love you. I still think about you. I still care about you.

Maybe we cannot always be together physically, but you are always close to my heart.

Distance separates us, but it cannot separate my love for you.

Someday, I hope we won't have to say
"I miss you from far away" anymore. 🥺
